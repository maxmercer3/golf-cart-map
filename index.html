DATA & SAFETY

What this map means

This is a conservative planning aid for standard golf carts (designed for golf-course/recreational use and not capable of more than 20 mph), not LSVs. Green appears only where the project can tie a corridor to an official municipal designation. Red is a warning layer, not a complete inventory of every prohibited road.

Important: A 25 mph speed limit does not by itself make a street legal. Municipal designation, posted signs, current law, and private-property rules still apply.

Current findings

  • Oakland’s 2024 ordinance generally prohibits golf carts except on roadways or communities approved by resolution.
  • Oakland Avenue has no legal longitudinal travel; the Town identifies crossings at Pollard, Tubb, and Winter’s Landing.
  • Winter Garden uses a designated downtown district. Sidewalks and the West Orange Trail are not golf-cart routes.
  • Hammock Preserve Loop, the rest of Oakland Park, and all community exits are HOA-permitted for golf carts, based on the resident’s confirmation supplied November 13, 2026.
  • Oakland Park’s Oakland and Winter Garden portions directly adjoin. The municipal boundary inside the neighborhood does not create a route gap or require crossing another road.
  • Winter Garden’s January 8, 2026 expansion added the Civitas-to-Plant chain: Tildenville School Road, Brick Road, West Story Road, and West Plant Street to South Park Avenue.
