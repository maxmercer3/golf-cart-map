# CartPath

Mobile-first planning map for standard golf carts in Winter Garden and Oakland, Florida.

The map distinguishes municipal corridors, restricted roads, authorized Oakland Avenue crossings, and Oakland Park HOA roads. Oakland Park roads and exits are treated as golf-cart accessible based on the resident's November 13, 2026 confirmation. Oakland Avenue remains crossing-only, including the resident-confirmed crossing into McKinnon Square.

The preferred downtown corridor uses the January 2026 Winter Garden expansion: Civitas Way to Tildenville School Road, Brick Road, West Story Road, and West Plant Street into the established downtown district. This is preferred over Bay Street to reduce speed bumps and maximize Plant Street travel.

Oakland Park is modeled as one continuous HOA-approved golf-cart network spanning the Oakland/Winter Garden municipal boundary. From Hammock Preserve Loop, the internal connection reaches Civitas Way through Eaglescrest Drive without crossing a separate public road. There is no legal or physical gap at the jurisdiction line.

Open `dist/index.html` through a web server. Browser geolocation requires HTTPS (or localhost) and user permission.
